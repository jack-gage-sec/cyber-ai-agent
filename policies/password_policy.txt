Owner:

1. Purpose

This policy defines authentication requirements to protect organizational accounts from unauthorized access.


2. Scope

This policy applies to all users, service accounts, applications, and systems requiring authentication.


3. Password Requirements

Passwords must meet the following requirements:

- Minimum length of 14 characters.
- Must not contain commonly used passwords.
- Must not be shared between users.
- Must be stored using approved cryptographic protections.


4. Multi-Factor Authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for:

- Administrative accounts.
- Remote access.
- Cloud applications containing sensitive information.
- Security management systems.


5. Account Protection

Accounts must be protected through:

- Account lockout controls.
- Monitoring of failed authentication attempts.
- Removal of inactive accounts.


6. Service Accounts

Service accounts must:

- Have documented ownership.
- Use unique credentials.
- Avoid interactive login unless approved.
